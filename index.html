              The SI Stylebook

              Plain words for a new name.

              How every site in our network uses AI, agent, AGI and superintelligence. Short enough to keep handy.

              Artificial intelligence (AI)
              Software that learns patterns from data to predict, write, see or decide. Still the word most people search for.We keep it next to SI in titles, so you can find us.
              AI agent
              An AI system that takes actions toward a goal, such as clicking, calling tools or sending messages, instead of only answering.We say "agent" only when it acts.
              AGI
              Artificial general intelligence: a system that matches people across most kinds of work. Nobody has shown one.We write "claims AGI" when a company says it, and stamp the claim like any rumor.
              Superintelligence (SI)
              The next generation of artificial intelligence (AI), and the official US federal name for it since Executive Order 14355.SI is a name, not a verdict. We never use it to say machines now outthink people.

              When we use each: SI in our names and headlines, with AI beside it. Agent when something acts. AGI only inside a quoted claim.
