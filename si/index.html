The order in one line

Executive Order 14434
“Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence”, signed September 29, 2026, published in the Federal Register October 2, 2026.
In plain words
A naming rule for federal agencies, defined by pointing at the existing legal definition of AI. It is not a claim that any machine is superintelligent.

How we keep this honest

  • Primary sources first. We link to the Federal Register and the full text of the order, not to someone’s summary.
  • Neutral tone. We describe what the order says. We do not praise it, criticise it, or take a side in any political argument.
  • Real numbers only. Statistics come from our newsroom’s reading, are rounded down so they never overstate, and carry an as-of date.
  • Analysis is labelled. Scenarios are scenarios, not predictions.
