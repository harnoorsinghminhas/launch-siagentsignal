Privacy Notice

Last updated October 3, 2026

We collect as little as we can, we use it to send you what you asked for, and we never sell it. This notice covers every site in the network and our emails. It is short and it means what it says.

1. Who we are

Superintelligence Media™ (the trade name of a Virginia limited liability company) publishes the sites listed in our Terms of Use. In this notice, “we” and “us” mean Superintelligence.

Postal address: available on request by replying to any email we sent you; a mailing address will be listed here once our mailbox is set up.

2. What we collect

When you join a list, we collect:

  • Your email address, so we can confirm it and send what you asked for.
  • The site you signed up on, so we know which newsletter or product you asked for.
  • Your time zone, so messages arrive at a sensible hour for you.
  • The page you landed on and any campaign tags in the link that brought you (for example a source or campaign label), so we can tell which of our own pages and links work.
  • Optional details you choose to add, such as a role or topics. You can leave these out.

Our sign-up service may also record the website that referred you, your device type, an approximate country, and a shortened form of your IP address. SI News Daily has an optional box for a mobile number, which we keep only if you also tick a separate text-message box; we do not ask for your phone number on our other sites. We do not ask for card details on our own pages: when checkout opens, payment is handled by Stripe and card details never touch our servers.

Like any website, the services that deliver our pages (GitHub Pages and Amazon Web Services) see standard server logs such as your IP address and browser type when you visit.

3. Why we use it

  • To confirm your email address and send the newsletters and messages you asked for.
  • To keep the sites running, safe and free of abuse.
  • To learn, in aggregate, which stories and pages people find useful, so we know what to write more of.

We do not use your details for advertising and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. Browser storage on your device

Some pages save your preferences and picks (for example a role you chose, or your answers in a game) in your browser’s local storage. That data stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you submit a form. You can clear it at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings. When you confirm your email address, our sign-up service may set one first-party session cookie so it can recognise you on your account page. It is not used for advertising.

There are no advertising or analytics trackers on our pages.

5. Email open and click counting

Our newsletter emails can include a tiny image and links that pass through our own redirect, so we can count opens and clicks for each edition. These counts are estimates, because some mail apps load images automatically. We use them only to decide what to write more of.

You can switch this off on your account page, or ask us to switch it off by replying to any email we sent you. When it is off, we do not record opens or clicks for you. Unsubscribe links are never tracked.

6. Fonts

Most of our sites load their typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches a font, Google receives your IP address and browser details, as it would for any web request. Where a page hosts its own fonts, no request goes to Google for them.

7. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers to run the sites and send email, including hosting (GitHub Pages and Amazon Web Services), email delivery (Amazon SES) and payments (Stripe). They process data only to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

8. Unsubscribe, access and deletion

  • Unsubscribe in one click from any email we send. We keep only a minimal record so that we do not email you again.
  • Ask us to delete your data, or to show you what we hold, by replying to any email we sent you and putting “Privacy request” in the subject line. If you no longer have an email from us, write to the contact address in the footer of the site you used.
  • We act on requests promptly and tell you when it is done.

9. Children

Our sites are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Happy Snowflake is for parents and teachers: please do not enter a child’s name, age, email or photo on any of our sites. If you believe a child has given us their details, tell us and we will delete them.

10. Visitors from outside the United States

Our sites are operated from the United States and your data is processed and stored there. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another place with data-protection rights, you may ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, and you may object to how we use it. Contact us as described above. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

11. Contact

Reply to any email we have sent you and put “Privacy request” in the subject line. Our postal address is available on request, as set out in section 1.
